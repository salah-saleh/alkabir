The 3 pillars of shipping AI-era products

Pillar 1 · Foundation

Solid engineering, data included

End-to-end product work — backend, frontend, the infrastructure underneath it, and the data layer that any AI feature will eventually depend on. Without this, AI features have nothing reliable to run on, no matter how impressive the demo looks.

Pillar 2 · Leverage

Automation & agentic workflows

There's a real difference between simple automation (fixed steps, no judgment calls) and agentic workflows (an LLM making decisions and using tools along the way) — picking the right one for the job matters. Without this, your team is still doing by hand the work AI should be doing for them: triaging tickets, answering repeat questions, running multi-step tasks across your tools.

Pillar 3 · Experience

Seamless UI & product design

A powerful backend and clever automation mean nothing if the product is confusing to use. This pillar covers interface and product design that feel obvious — including how AI-generated answers get grounded in your real data (not confident guessing) and surfaced in a way people actually trust and adopt.
